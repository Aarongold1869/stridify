🏃 Stridify

Running Playlists Synced to Your Cadence

Stridify creates personalized music playlists that match your running speed. By calculating your running cadence (steps per minute) and converting it to BPM, we build playlists that keep you in rhythm.

Features

  • Cadence-based BPM calculation
  • Personalized track recommendations
  • Spotify integration
  • Genre and energy-based filtering
